## A note on stock sound in this app

This is the only stock audio here, and it is a placeholder.

Openly-licensed celebration sound barely exists — Wikimedia Commons has one
applause clip and no madal, no damphu, no Nepali folk, nothing of Ilam. Every
good library (Freesound, Pixabay) sits behind a login.

That's fine, because stock sound is the wrong answer anyway. The single best
recording for this hall is **the Rais actually singing** at Dipisha's birthday —
Mummy's voice, the tin roof, everyone off-key. Ten seconds on a phone in Asar
beats any sound library ever made. Replace this file the moment you have it.
